# Marvel United: Campaign Sketches

Sketch overview of branched story campaigns — not fully written, just a planning reference. Each campaign targets **2–3 expansions maximum** to keep the barrier to entry reasonable. Expansions referenced are based on the character relationships in [OscarGarPer/Marvel-United-Json-Database](https://github.com/OscarGarPer/Marvel-United-Json-Database).

---

## Revised Existing Campaigns

The two campaigns already in this repository were trimmed to a more playable footprint. Original expansion counts are noted in parentheses.

| Campaign Name | # of Expansions | List of Expansions | Notes |
|---|:---:|---|---|
| **Earth's Mightiest: "Shadows Over New York"** | 3 *(was 7)* | Marvel United (Avengers Core) · Enter the Spider-Verse · Rise of the Black Panther | Cut Tales of Asgard, Return of the Sinister Six, Season 1 SGs, Infinity Gauntlet, Annihilation. Keeps Iron Man / Cap / Black Widow / Hulk vs Red Skull / Ultron / Taskmaster, with Spider-Man joining mid-campaign and a Wakanda branch via Killmonger. |
| **X-Men: "From the Ashes"** | 3 *(was 6)* | Marvel United: X-Men · Days of Future Past · The Horsemen of Apocalypse | Cut X-Men First Class, Blue Team, Season 2 SGs. Keeps Cyclops / Jean Grey / Wolverine / Storm vs Magneto / Brotherhood, Sentinels / Nimrod in the dark path, and Apocalypse as the final boss. |
